WoodDessign shall provide structural analysis and wood-member design through a clean design-standard interface. The NDS 
engine shall maximize reuse of verified/applicable open-source functionality identified in the approved reuse matrix. GPT-2 shall 
not recreate reusable functionality without a documented reason.
The calculation layer shall remain separate from product/material data, product selection, reporting, entitlement, and 
application/UI concerns.

7. Input Schema Requirements
Inputs shall be typed, unit-aware and validated. Minimum groups: Project; design standard/edition/unit system; Member; 
material/product; geometry; support conditions; unbraced/effective lengths; service condition; treatment/incising; load cases; 
load locations/distributions; load combinations; serviceability limits; and product-selection constraints where applicable.
Material/product records shall carry provenance fields including category, manufacturer where applicable, designation, 
species/material class, grade/stress class, dimensions, section properties, strength/stiffness values, density, design 
standard/edition/method, source document/table/page/date, units and verification status.
8. Calculation Workflow
1. Validate design standard, edition, units and calculation applicability.
2. Validate geometry, material/product record and required service-condition inputs.
3. Generate/receive structural demands from the analysis engine.
4. Retrieve approved reference properties.
5. Determine applicable adjustment/modification factors independently for each design property.
6. Calculate adjusted design properties.
7. Perform strength/stability/serviceability checks.
8. Retain all intermediate values required for engineering review.
9. Determine governing utilization and controlling load combination/condition.
10. Return structured results, PASS/FAIL, warnings, limitations and traceability.
11. Where product selection is requested, reject any candidate failing any mandatory check and rank only compatible passing 
candidates by the approved objective.
9. Decision Logic and Safety Requirements
Unsupported conditions shall fail closed. No exception handler, lookup fallback or UI default may convert an unsupported 
engineering case into an apparently valid result. Strength and serviceability checks remain separate. NDS and CSA O86 
calculations remain separate. Candidates from incompatible standards, editions or design methods shall not be compared.
10. Output Register Requirements
Outputs shall include analysis demands, reference properties, every applied adjustment factor, adjusted properties, intermediate
stability/slenderness values, resistances/capacities, deflections and limits, demand/capacity or utilization ratios, governing case, 
PASS/FAIL, warnings, invalid-input messages, limitations and source/formula/test identifiers.

18. Multi-Ply Beam Scope — Closed Decision, No HOLD
A side-by-side multi-ply beam shall NOT be assumed to act as a fully composite monolithic section solely because the plies are 
fastened together. For WoodDessign v1, each ply is designed using the load actually assigned to that ply. Total member 
capacity may be assembled only from the sum of individually verified ply capacities under a load-distribution model that does not
rely on unverified composite stiffness. Where the application requires load transfer among plies, the multi-ply connection shall 
be checked under the 2024 NDS connection provisions. This is an explicit production scope rule, not a HOLD item.

20. Invalid-Input and Fail-Closed Rules
• RB > 50: invalid member for NDS beam-stability procedure; return FAIL/INVALID, never extrapolate.
• le/d > 50 for permanent column design: FAIL/INVALID.
• D1 <= 0 or D2 <= 0 in combined compression-bending: FAIL due to instability/overstress; never clamp denominator.
• Unknown load-duration, LRFD time-effect, load-type, factor applicability, size class or product record: validation error.
• Bearing Cb shall not be applied outside its stated location/length applicability.

(for example assumed full-composite multi-ply beam action without a verified connection model, or CSA O86 equations) are 
explicit scope boundaries.
